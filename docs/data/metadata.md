# 🗒️ Metadata

## Overview
Every FungiTastic observation comes with **rich tabular metadata** describing not just the specimen, but also its context and collection details.

---

## Fields Included

- **Observation date:** yyyy-mm-dd, plus year, month, and day columns.
- **EXIF camera metadata:** exposure, color space, device type, etc.
- **Habitat:** E.g., Mixed woodland, Deciduous woodland (32 options).
- **Substrate:** Where the specimen grows (e.g., Bark, Soil, Stone).
- **Taxonomic labels:** Full hierarchy from species up to kingdom.
- **Toxicity status:** Edible or poisonous (binary label).
- **Geolocation:** Latitude, longitude, plus region/district/country.
- **Biogeographical zone:** Atlantic, Continental, Alpine, etc.
- **Elevation:** Standardized meters above sea level.
- **Land cover:** Classification from MODIS (e.g., savanna, barren).

---

## Usage Notes

- Nearly all observations (>99.9%) have full metadata.
- Metadata is crucial for multi-modal learning, habitat modeling, and context-aware classification.
